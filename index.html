The most important thing it does is stop

An assistant that guesses will eventually promise a price you do not charge or a table you do not have. Ours is built to hand the conversation over instead.

  • It will not confirm a booking. A person does that, so nothing gets double-promised.
  • It will not quote a price you have not given us. No menu item, no answer.
  • It will not pretend to know. It says so plainly and passes the customer on.

The things owners ask first

Short answers, including the ones that are not entirely in our favour.

Will it use my current WhatsApp number?

We would recommend not. A number connected to this system can no longer be used in the normal WhatsApp app. That is Meta's rule, not ours, so we set it up on a separate number and your existing one keeps working exactly as it does today.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so and passes the customer to a person. It is built to answer only from the information you give us, and it will never invent a price or confirm a booking.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It takes the repetitive questions off whoever is holding the phone during service. Anything that needs a person still gets one.

Can it reply in Dhivehi?

Yes. Dhivehi and English, replying in whichever language the customer writes in.

How much does it cost?

A one-off setup and a small monthly fee that covers running it and keeping your information current. The exact figure depends on your message volume, so get in touch and we will look at it properly.

How long until it's working?

We can usually show you a working version with your real menu in about two weeks. Going live takes a few weeks more, because Meta runs a business verification process whose speed we do not control. We will always tell you where it stands.

Something we have not covered? Ask us directly and we will answer honestly, including when the answer is that this is not right for you.
