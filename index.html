Questions

The things owners ask first

Will it use my current WhatsApp number?

We'd recommend not. A number connected to this system can't be used in the normal WhatsApp app any more — that's Meta's rule, not ours. So we set it up on a separate number, and your existing one keeps working exactly as it does today.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so and passes the customer to a person. It's built to answer only from the information you give us — it will never invent a price or confirm a booking.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It takes the repetitive questions off whoever's holding the phone during service. Anything that needs a person still gets one.

Can it reply in Dhivehi?

Yes — Dhivehi and English, replying in whichever language the customer writes in.

How much does it cost?

A one-off setup and a small monthly fee that covers running it and keeping your information current. The exact figure depends on your message volume — get in touch and we'll look at it properly.

How long until it's working?

We can usually show you a working version with your real menu in about two weeks. Going live takes a few weeks more, because Meta runs a business verification process we don't control the speed of. We'll always tell you where it actually stands.
