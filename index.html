The most important thing it does is stop

An assistant that guesses will eventually promise a price you don't charge or a table you don't have. Ours is built to hand the conversation over instead.

  • It won't confirm a booking. A person does that, so nothing gets double-promised.
  • It won't quote a price you haven't given us. No menu item, no answer.
  • It won't pretend to know. It says so plainly and passes the customer on.

The things owners ask first

Will it use my current WhatsApp number?

We'd recommend not. A number connected to this system can't be used in the normal WhatsApp app any more. That's Meta's rule, not ours, so we set it up on a separate number and your existing one keeps working exactly as it does today.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so and passes the customer to a person. It's built to answer only from the information you give us, and it will never invent a price or confirm a booking.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It takes the repetitive questions off whoever's holding the phone during service. Anything that needs a person still gets one.

Can it reply in Dhivehi?

Yes. Dhivehi and English, replying in whichever language the customer writes in.

How much does it cost?

A one-off setup and a small monthly fee that covers running it and keeping your information current. The exact figure depends on your message volume — get in touch and we'll look at it properly.

How long until it's working?

We can usually show you a working version with your real menu in about two weeks. Going live takes a few weeks more, because Meta runs a business verification process whose speed we don't control. We'll always tell you where it actually stands.

Something we haven't covered? Ask us directly — we'll answer honestly, including when the answer is that this isn't right for you.
